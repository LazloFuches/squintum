Robert Egert (b. 1958, Brooklyn) is a painter whose work spans four decades, moving between figuration, abstraction, and abstract narrative. He received his BFA from Pratt Institute and an MLA from the City University of New York Center for Cultural Studies. Egert began exhibiting in the early 1980s at Civilian Warfare, White Columns, and Holly Solomon in New York. His subsequent projects have explored themes of environmental crisis, posthumanism, and the boundaries between the organic and the constructed, drawing on sources from Ovid to Octavia Butler. Major series include Morphopolis, Anthropocene, Gothic Interiors, Nocturnes, and Small Worlds. His paintings are held in private and institutional collections in the United States and Europe.
